# Agent directives

Generated by `cargo dacc emit all`; `cargo dacc emit all --check` verifies
this file is fresh. Do not edit it by hand — the generator is the source.

## Navigation

- `cargo dacc map [--format json]` — the registry map: thrusts, slices,
  works with journal states, and counters.
- `cargo dacc where --file <path> [--format json]` — a place in code: its
  `#[doc_anchor]` markup, the documents citing it, the related works.
- `cargo dacc work state [<w-slug>]` and `cargo dacc metrics` — work states
  and counters from the journal fold.
- Do not navigate the registry with find, grep or ls; ripgrep is allowed
  for sources only.

## Rituals

- Every change has a basis in the plan: a work item with an origin.
- `cargo dacc work next [--format json]` admits the next task under the
  journal lock and writes its `started` event; the `wip_limit` setting
  refuses overflow with the `wip-limit` code.
- Commits go through `cargo dacc commit -F <message-file> -- <paths>`: the
  subject is `[TYPE](scope): summary`, and the trailer is
  `Dacc-Work: w-slug`.
- `cargo dacc work land <w-slug>` lands with a proof on the tree; a defect
  fix — the Divergence origin — or an irreversible change lands only with
  `--red-before <test>`; `--mutation-proof <check>` and
  `--anti-vacuum <subject>` state the other anti-vacuous proofs.
- A slice closes on its outcome: `cargo dacc slice close <s-slug>`.
